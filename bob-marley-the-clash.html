Bob Marley and the Clash

Rock band The Clash gained popularity as part of the UK punk scene in the 1970s. Despite being one of the frontrunners of punk, they took eclectic influences from genres such as ska and hip-hop as well. As seen below, they are also heavily influence by reggae and the work of one of its pioneers, Bob Marley.
