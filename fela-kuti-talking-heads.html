Fela Kuti and Talking Heads

Talking Heads pioneered the new wave genre by blending classic American rock with punk and funk in the 1970s. They were heavily influenced by afrobeats founding father Fela Kuti, especially his complex polyrhythms, basslines, and song structures. Their 1980 album "Remain in Light" paid much tribute to Fela, and still remains in the spotlight today.

Interview Transcript: David Byrne on Fela Kuti

This interview showcases David Byrne (Talking Heads frontman)'s thoughts and inspirations from Fela Kuti. This was published as a sidebar interview for a larger project on Fela Kuti and those he influenced. From here, Talking Heads then influenced many new, post-new wave genres like synth pop, modern alternative rock, and modern goth rock. Bands such as Radiohead, Arcade Fire, and LCD Soundsystem (see here) can trace their roots back to afrobeats king Fela Kuti.
