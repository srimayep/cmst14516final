Hi! My name is Srimaye Peddinti and I compiled this exhibit for the course CMST 14516: Black British Cinema and the Speculative Archive at the University of Chicago.

This course was taught by Prof. Ally Field as part of the London: Theorizing the Present September term abroad in 2026, in partnership with the Chicago Center for Contemporary Theory (3CT).

Feel free to email me at srimaye [at] uchicago [dot] edu with any questions, concerns, comments etc.

Thank you to the professor, assistants, and fellow students for a great study abroad experience!
