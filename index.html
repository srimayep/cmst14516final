Black influence on contemporary indie music: a selection

For this online exhibit, I've chosen to pair together major indie music artists with their core influences, spanning multiple genres from techno to trip hop. This selection is meant to expose the viewer to the ubiquity of black influence in today's most pervasive subgenres, and the different means in which it pervades them.
